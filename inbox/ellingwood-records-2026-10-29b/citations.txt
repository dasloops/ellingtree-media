KEY POINTS
- Israel Ellingwood married Susanna Rand at Boston, 18 May 1741 (typed Registrar's
  register). His will is dated 2 Nov 1776 and was proved 19 Mar 1785.
- In 1785 Enoch James became guardian of Ralph, William, Joseph and Richard, minor
  children of Israel's son Ralph. The Cross Street tenements and the Beverly land were
  settled on the son Ralph, who died in 1786 (Paul Revere a surety and appraiser).
- 19 Apr 1787: Robert Hichborn of Boston sold "Richard Ellingwood of Boston ... Cooper" a
  100-acre lot on the west side of the Penobscot and half his salmon-fishery right
  (Lincoln 22:93). 3 Apr 1792: John Ripley of Boston sold "Ralph Ellengwood of
  Wheelsborough" the next lot, bounded on Richard's land, with the same kind of fishery
  right. 3 Sept 1796: Ralph "of Hampden" and his wife Delilah sold that lot; Richard
  witnessed.
- Richard Ellingwood of Frankfort, "Trader" or "Merchant", was buying and selling land
  from 1797 to 1803 (grantor and grantee indexes). His first indexed Frankfort purchases
  (Hancock 6:464-466, Dec 1798 to Mar 1799) include 100 acres "near Bald Hill Cove" from
  Thomas Holbrooks for $700. No Ellingwood deed is indexed at 4:509. By 19 Apr 1804 he was dead and Hope Ellingwood was
  his widow and "sole Executrix".
- Docket 305 (1804-05): Hope guardian of Richard and John (over 14) and Sally, Ralph,
  Susanna and Lucy (under 14); Ralph Ellingwood a surety.
- 4 Nov 1807 (typed 1909 copies of Hancock 23:286-287): Hope, widow, and Ralph and Delilah
  sold their shares in the estate of "John H. Shaw late of Frankfort", named as the
  father of Hope and of Delilah. 28 Dec 1802 (12:136): John Holdershaw of Frankfort gave
  John Holdershaw Junior the acre with the store on Bald Hill Cove. 16 Sept 1807
  (23:159-160): "John Holdershaw of Boston ... Mariner" quitclaimed that acre and store, a
  house and barn, and other land to Asa Bean; one witness is typed "J. H. Shaw".
- July Term 1829: the Waldo Court of Common Pleas gave William Prescott judgment against
  "Hope Ellingwood of Frankfort ... Widow", who defaulted, on her note of 30 Sept 1811.
- 5 July 1813 (Hancock 39:158): Hope, "widow of Richard Ellingwood, deceased", Richard
  Ellingwood, and Moses B. Patten and his wife Sarah, "heirs of said deceased, & all of
  Frankfort", sold part of the homestead farm. In the 1818 deeds of the same estate
  (39:157-159) the grantors are Hope, Susan, the Pattens and Paul D. and Lucy Delano.
- March Term 1875 (Kankakee County Court): Finley Ellingwood, guardian since 27 Jan
  1875, was ordered to sell the minor Ellsworth Ellingwood's one-fifth interest in Lot 8
  and the south half of Lot 7, Block 69, Associates Addition, Kankakee. The order does
  not say how Ellsworth came by it.
- 23 April 1917: Richard Everett Ellingwood, student, born at Evanston 14 Feb 1895,
  applied for a passport for Y.M.C.A. work in France and Great Britain, sailing from New
  York on the Espagne on 5 May 1917. He gave his father as Finley Ellingwood, born in
  Manchester, Indiana.
- 9 June 1777: the Council ordered a letter-of-marque commission for "Richd Ellinwood",
  master of Thomas Adams's schooner Cleora, bound for the French West Indies.
