    Ralph Ellinwood to Israel & Ralph Ellinwood          Recd on Record March 4th 1737
    Know all Men by these presents That I Ralph Ellinwood of Beverly in ye County of Essex
    in New England Husbandman in consideration of ye Just Sum of Forty pounds in Bills of
    publick Credit to me in hand paid before ye Delivery hereof by Israel Ellinwood of
    Boston in ye County of Suffolk in New England aforesaid Joyner & Ralph Ellinwood Junr
    of Beverly aforesd Coaster Have given granted bargained sold and do by these presents
    give grant bargain sell alien and fully freely and absolutely convey and confirm unto
    them ye sd Israel Ellinwood & Ralph Ellinwood Junr their heirs and Assigns forever in
    egal proportion for Quantity and Quality a Certain Tract of Land Scituate in Beverly
    aforesaid being the Same on which the house built by their father my Son Ralph
    Ellinwood Decd now Stands, containing about one quarter of an acre with the flatts and
    Beach adjoyning thereto to Lowwater mark and is bounded Westerly on Land late Samuel
    Ellinwood Decd nine poles & half and so on ye same Course to Lowwater mark, Northerly
    upon a Private Highway eight poles, Easterly upon my own Land three poles and an half,
    & Southerly upon ye Harbour eight poles or however otherwise butted bounded or reputed
    to be bounded. To Have & To Hold ... [the usual covenants] ... In Witness whereof I Do
    Hereunto Set my hand and Seal this Third Day of March Anno Domini One Thousand Seven
    Hundred and Thirty Seven/eight and in the Eleventh year of the Reign of our Sovereign
    Lord George the Second by ye Grace of God King &c
    Signed Sealed & D in presence of            Ralph Ellinwood & a Seal
      Wm Ellinwood, Thos Hardee
    Essex ss Beverly March 3d 1737/8 Received of Israel and Ralph Ellinwood Junr ye
    Consideration abovementioned by me Ralph Ellinwood
    Essex ss Beverly March 4th 1737/8 Then Ralph Ellinwood abovenamed appeared personally
    and owned ye abovewritten Instrument to be his free act & Deed  Coram Robert Hale Just
    Pacis
  Notes:     Ralph Jr. (the husbandman, d. 1741) sells to the two eldest sons of his son
             Ralph, deceased, the quarter acre on Beverly harbour where their father's house
             stands. It calls Israel "of Boston ... Joyner" in 1738 (the 1743 acquittance in
             batch 1 calls him "Coaster") and the younger Ralph "Ralph Ellinwood Junr of
             Beverly ... Coaster". Matches the task on the father, the grandsons and the
             place.
