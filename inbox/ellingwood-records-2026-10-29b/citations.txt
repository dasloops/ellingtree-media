deed-1653-wenham-garvin-ellingwood.jpg              [item 7]
  Collection: Essex. Deeds 1639-1658, v. 1-3 (as above)
  Image:     https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/3:1:3QS7-L9ZZ-BCR4
             image 62 of 763, DGS 007462653
  Held by:   as above
  Header:    pages 40 (left) and 41 (right) of the fair copy; original page 17 in the margin
  Where:     right page, the second entry
  Transcription:
    Wenham  30th of the first moneth 1653
    Robt Gowing [?] hath sold unto Raph Ellingwood his howses in Wenham in the County of
    Essex wth eight acres of land Joyning to yt and ten acres of land in the neck and towe
    acres of medow in the great medow nere the Iland & all the Comon wch lyes in Wenham
    wch belongs to the same house for and in Consideration of nyne pounds as by a deed
    dated the 24 day of the 11 [?] moneth 1652.
  Notes:     UNSURE OF IDENTITY. The buyer's name and the date (March 1653, three months
             before the Skerry sale) fit Ralph of Salem, but nothing here names a wife,
             child or Salem; Wenham is the next town north of Beverly. Brought in so the
             owner can judge. The seller's surname is uncertain (Gowing or Garvin; the
             full-text index reads it "Garvin").

deed-1738-beverly-ellinwood.jpg              [item 7]
  Collection: Essex. Deeds 1737-1739, v. 74-76
  Record:    none indexed (Full-Text search, "Ellinwood Narragansett", 1727-1745, hit
             this page)
  Image:     https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/3:1:3QSQ-G9ZZ-1FBF
             image 441 of 829, DGS 007463289
  Held by:   Essex County (Massachusetts). Register of Deeds
  Header:    right page numbered 131; the volume (74, 75 or 76) is not shown on the image
  Where:     left page from the heading "Ralph Ellinwood to Israel & Ralph Ellinwood" to the
             foot, and the top of the right page
  Transcription:
    Ralph Ellinwood to Israel & Ralph Ellinwood          Recd on Record March 4th 1737
    Know all Men by these presents That I Ralph Ellinwood of Beverly in ye County of Essex
    in New England Husbandman in consideration of ye Just Sum of Forty pounds in Bills of
    publick Credit to me in hand paid before ye Delivery hereof by Israel Ellinwood of
    Boston in ye County of Suffolk in New England aforesaid Joyner & Ralph Ellinwood Junr
    of Beverly aforesd Coaster Have given granted bargained sold and do by these presents
    give grant bargain sell alien and fully freely and absolutely convey and confirm unto
    them ye sd Israel Ellinwood & Ralph Ellinwood Junr their heirs and Assigns forever in
    egal proportion for Quantity and Quality a Certain Tract of Land Scituate in Beverly
    aforesaid being the Same on which the house built by their father my Son Ralph
    Ellinwood Decd now Stands, containing about one quarter of an acre with the flatts and
    Beach adjoyning thereto to Lowwater mark and is bounded Westerly on Land late Samuel
    Ellinwood Decd nine poles & half and so on ye same Course to Lowwater mark, Northerly
    upon a Private Highway eight poles, Easterly upon my own Land three poles and an half,
    & Southerly upon ye Harbour eight poles or however otherwise butted bounded or reputed
    to be bounded. To Have & To Hold ... [the usual covenants] ... In Witness whereof I Do
    Hereunto Set my hand and Seal this Third Day of March Anno Domini One Thousand Seven
    Hundred and Thirty Seven/eight and in the Eleventh year of the Reign of our Sovereign
    Lord George the Second by ye Grace of God King &c
    Signed Sealed & D in presence of            Ralph Ellinwood & a Seal
      Wm Ellinwood, Thos Hardee
    Essex ss Beverly March 3d 1737/8 Received of Israel and Ralph Ellinwood Junr ye
    Consideration abovementioned by me Ralph Ellinwood
    Essex ss Beverly March 4th 1737/8 Then Ralph Ellinwood abovenamed appeared personally
    and owned ye abovewritten Instrument to be his free act & Deed  Coram Robert Hale Just
    Pacis
  Notes:     Ralph Jr. (the husbandman, d. 1741) sells to the two eldest sons of his son
             Ralph, deceased, the quarter acre on Beverly harbour where their father's house
             stands. It calls Israel "of Boston ... Joyner" in 1738 (the 1743 acquittance in
             batch 1 calls him "Coaster") and the younger Ralph "Ralph Ellinwood Junr of
             Beverly ... Coaster". Matches the task on the father, the grandsons and the
             place.

  Transcription:
    Thomas Ellinwood & al Exrs &c to Ralph Ellinwood       Recd on Record Octr 31st 1741
    Know all Men by these Presents That Thomas Ellinwood of Woodstock in the County of
    Worcester Joiner and Joseph Trask of Beverly in the County of Essex Blacksmith
    Executors of the last Will & Testament of Ralph Ellinwood late of Beverly afd
    Husbandman Deceased bearing date the Eighth Day of April AD 1741 Pursuant to the Power
    & Authority to them granted in & by the sd Will by the sd Ralph Ellinwood For & in
    consideration of the sum of Twenty six pounds in Bills of credit to them in hand paid
    for the Uses & Purposes in said Will mentioned by Ralph Ellinwood of said Beverly
    Coaster the receipt whereof they hereby acknowledge Have Bargained & Sold ... unto the
    said Ralph Ellinwood his Heirs & Assigns forever All that Certain parcel of Land
    scituate in Beverly aforesaid containing Thirteen poles (whereof the said Ralph
    Ellinwood Decd Died seized) Bounded Notherly on a private Way, Easterly on land of
    Ebenezer Ellinwood, Southerly on ye Way that leads to the Water as the Fence stands, &
    Westerly on land of the aforesaid Ralph Ellinwood, with the Priviledges &
    Appurtenances thereto belonging ... [covenants] ... In Witness whereof they the said
    Thomas Ellinwood & Joseph Trask Executors as aforesaid have hereto set their hands &
    seals the Thirtieth Day of October Anno Domini 1741.
    Signed Sealed & Dd in presence of us          Thomas Ellinwood & a seal
      John Higginson, John Higginson Junr         Joseph Trask & a seal
    Essex ss Octr 30th 1741 Thomas Ellinwood and Joseph Trask Exrs aforesd owned this to
    be their Act & Deed before me John Higginson J Peace
  Notes:     The executors named in Ralph Jr.'s will of 8 April 1741 (batch 1) sell to his
             grandson Ralph, coaster, the small lot the will ordered sold: bounded east on
             Ebenezer Ellinwood's land and south on "ye Way that leads to the Water", as in
             the will. It gives the executor son as "Thomas Ellinwood of Woodstock in the
             County of Worcester Joiner".
