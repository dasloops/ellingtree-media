birth-record-1844-waldrohrbach-borell.jpg              [item 1]
  Collection: Germany, Bavaria, Catholic and Lutheran Church Records, 1591-1963
             (browsed film; this entry is not indexed)
  Image:     https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/3:1:3Q9M-CSJB-896D-T
             image 499 of 597, DGS 008110734 (Katholische Kirche Waldhambach, Kirchenbuch:
             Taufen, Heiraten, Tote 1735-1858, 1772-1858; FHL film 400459)
  Held by:   Stadtarchiv Speyer (filmed by the Genealogical Society of Utah, 1961)
  Header:    Baptisms of Waldrohrbach (the Waldrohrbach section of the Waldhambach books),
             right-hand page 105 [stamped 235], "1844", entry No. 4. Margin: "Ludovicus
             Guilielmus Borell 20 Febr."
  Where:     right-hand page, middle.
  Transcription (Latin):
    Anno Domini millesimo octingentesimo quadragesimo quarto, die vigesima Februarii natus
    et vigesima prima Februarii a me infra scripto parocho baptizatus est filius legitimus
    Jacobi Borell ludimagistri in Waldrohrbach et Elisabethae Bauer conjugum catholicorum,
    cui nomen Ludovicus Guilielmus impositum. Patrinus fuit Georgius Bauer civis in
    Waldrohrbach. Ita testor Laurentius Trapp parochus in Eschbach.
    [In the year of the Lord 1844, on 20 February was born, and on 21 February baptized by
    me the undersigned parish priest, the legitimate son of Jacob Borell, schoolteacher in
    Waldrohrbach, and Elisabetha Bauer, Catholic spouses, to whom the name Ludwig Wilhelm was
    given. The godfather was Georg Bauer, citizen of Waldrohrbach. So I attest, Laurentius
    Trapp, parish priest in Eschbach.]
  Notes:     Ludwig (Louis) Borell. Ties: father Jacob Borell, schoolteacher, and mother
             Elisabetha Bauer, the parents the Minnesota death index gives as "Jacob Borell,
             Barbara Bauer" (Louis Borell, d. Waverly 1936); Waldrohrbach, the place in the
             brief; the godfather Georg Bauer is of the mother's family name. The family book
             (above) lists him among the children who went to "Amerika". Born 20 Feb 1844,
             not 22 Feb.
