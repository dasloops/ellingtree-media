KEY POINTS
- All three records asked for were found, in the Plymouth Colony record books themselves (the
  bound colony books as filmed in 1968), not in later transcripts.
- The administration (5 June 1644) is in Court Orders, on p. 101, in the same hand as the
  surrounding court business: letters of administration to Jonathan and Love Brewster, and the
  inventory exhibited on their oaths.
- The inventory runs over seven images (fols. 53-59 of the Wills book). The goods total
  107li 0s 8d (signed Myles Standish, Tho: Prence). The Latin books total 15li 19s 4d. All
  the books together come to 42li 19s 11d, and goods and books together to 150li 00s 07d
  (signed Wm Bradford, Tho: Prence). The arithmetic checks: 28.08.10 + 78.11.10 = 107.00.08,
  and 107.00.08 + 42.19.11 = 150.00.07.
- "Perth Assembly" is on fol. 57 (image 5 of the folder).
- The 1645 award is in Deeds vol. 1, pp. 198-199. It ends "ffinished at Plymouth the xxth
  August 1645" and is signed William Bradford, Edw: Winslow, Tho: Prence, Myles Standish.
  P. 198 has "we gaue him his fathers Armes". The lower part of p. 198 is water-stained and
  its edge is torn, so the transcription below is incomplete there.
- I could not open Bowman's printed text in this session (the request to archive.org was not
  approved in time). So the readings below have NOT been compared with his, and where I could
  not read the hand I have marked [?] rather than borrow a printed reading.
