files marked SCREENSHOT OF INDEX ENTRY, where FamilySearch shows no image to ordinary users.
Square brackets mark readings that are uncertain or are explanations added by the transcriber.

The task items each file answers are given after the file name. A list of everything NOT
found, with the collections, places and spellings tried, is at the end.

KEY POINTS
- Edith Allee is Albert and Lucinda Allee's daughter. In 1880 Thomas Hatton is entered as
  Albert Allie's "son in law", living next door (census-1880-reynolds-hatten). Her 1912
  Chicago death record names her father Albert Allee (death-certificate-hayes-1912). Bertha's
  1913 licence names her mother "Edna Hatten whose maiden name was Edna Alley"
  (marriage-record-timm-hatten-1913).
- Edith/Edna Hayes died in Chicago on 15 Jan 1912, a widow, aged "50" (born 4 Dec 1858 per
  the index; the censuses say about 1853).
- Thomas J. Hatten was born about April-May 1850 in Ohio. He is a 1-month-old in Ralph and
  Sarah Ann Hatten's household in 1850 (census-1850-jackson-hatten). He died in St Paul, MN, on
  26 Dec 1924, aged 74. That death record names his parents Ralph and Sarah and his wife
  Mrs Mary Hatton.
- Benjamin Philbrick and Ruth Marvin married in Gallia Co., Ohio, on 3 May 1818.
- Stephen K. Brown married Caroline Williamson in McHenry Co., IL, on 21 Mar 1857.
- Ralph Hatten married Henrietta Browne in Seattle on 21 Feb 1912. This is where the Hatten
  and Brown lines join; S. A. Browne was a witness.
- In 1910 Samuel Hoopes's wife "Stina", aged 50 and born in Illinois, was living with her
  sister Carrie Brown, aged 33. This identifies S. Rena Hoopes as Stena/Ella Brown.
