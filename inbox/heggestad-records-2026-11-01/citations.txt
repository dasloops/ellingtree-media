Subject: the farm Heggestad ("Hæggestad"), Grøndalen, Hemsedal, Buskerud, Norway.
gnr. 86, bnr. 9, Hemsedal (kommune 0618 then, 3326 today). Address today: Grøndalsvegen 777,
3560 Hemsedal. OpenStreetMap node 9620052403 (name "Heggestad", place=farm,
60.9295861 N, 8.3984445 E, stedsnr 372613).
Family in question: Halvor Trondsen Heggestad (b. 17 Dec 1885, d. Hemsedal 21 Aug 1975),
unmarried, owner from about 1920; his parents Trond Halvorsen (1850-1894) and Kari
[Ingvars/Ingvalds]datter (b. 1859), who married secondly Syver (Sjugurd) Larsen of Stunde;
his sisters Anne (b. 1887), Kari (b. 1888), Margit (b. 1890); half-sisters Mari and
Margit Syversdatter ("Margit Heggestad", b. 8 Dec 1900, d. 17 Jun 1982).

KEY POINTS
- Heggestad's own folio in the Hemsedal land register (panteregister) is found: gnr. 86
  bnr. 9, "Hæggestad", løpenr. 669b, split off from løpenr. 669 Medgaarden. Side 315.
- 1884: the widow Anne Nilsdatter Haggestad deeded the farm to her son Trond Halvorsen.
- 1897: after Trond's death the estate settlement laid the farm out to his widow Kari
  (then remarried to Syver Larsen), with sums secured on it for the children Halvor,
  Anne, Kari and Margit. This names all four of Trond's children, matching the family.
- There is NO deed to Halvor around 1920 on the folio. His title is registered only in
  1958, by a declaration ("erklæring") that Halvor T. Heggestad holds grunnbok title.
  Rudvin (1976) p. 391 says Halvor took over the farm in 1910.
- HALVOR SOLD HEGGESTAD OUT OF THE FAMILY: Rudvin p. 391 says that in 1961 he sold the farm
  to Trond Kirkebøen (p. 267 says 1960). Kirkebøen built a new road and bridge to it and
  planned a cabin field (hyttefelt) on the property. The book gives no family tie between
  Kirkebøen and the Heggestads; elsewhere it shows him buying other Grøndalen farms for
  cabin development (Helgeseth, 1956, now Helgeseth Feriesenter). So unless it came back
  later, the farm was not Halvor's to leave when he died in 1975, nor Margit's in 1982.
- Halvor then lived at Bjørklund (gnr. 67 bnr. 86) near Trøym, in the centre of Hemsedal,
  and entered the old people's home Bygdaheimen in 1975.
- The 1958 grunnbok declaration came about three years before the sale, which fits
  formalising his title before selling (an inference, not stated in any source).
- "Vestenfor" in the register = Vestafør: Rudvin says the farm was also called
  Vestafør or Surre in everyday speech.
- Every owner shown 1884-1961 is Halvor's direct line (grandmother Anne, father Trond,
  mother Kari, Halvor). Rudvin says Halvor's grandfather Halvor Trondson (1820-1881) bought
  it from his father, the man at Medgarden; Norske gardsbruk (1948) says the farm had been
  in the family since about 1840.
- Who owns it NOW (after Kirkebøen) is still unknown: Kartverket's register was down
  (503, maintenance) on 2026-11-01.

Hemsedals slektshistorie 1693-1975                         [Rudvin 1976]
  Book:      Hans Flaten (1858-1930), with Ola Rudvin (1899-1978): Hemsedals slektshistorie
             1693-1975 (Hemsedal kommune, 1976). First edition 1925.
  Online:    https://www.nb.no/items/60f05aad1fb930f08fee6270001b50d7  (Norway only)
  Read:      2026-11-01, on screen. Search "Heggestad" gives hits on pp. 267, 271, 355, 357,
             391 (nb.no page ids _0273, _0277, _0361, _0363, _0397).
  The book uses the farm-name form "Heggjistad" and nynorsk dialect spellings.

  p. 391, the farm's own entry: Heggjistad, gnr 86, bnr 9, lnr 669 b; written form
    Heggestad. Also called Vestafør or Surre in everyday speech.
    - After Nils Arneson Høvdaé ("Hullern", 1767-1832-65) died, the farm was sold at auction.
      The man at Médgardé (Medgarden) bought it and later sold it to his son Halvor.
    - Halvor Trondson 1820-81-61 [b. 1820, d. 1881 aged 61] m. 1844 Anne Nilsdtr Stunde
      1820-87-67. Children: Birgit 1846-91-45 m. 1867 Ivar Rudningji; Barbo 1848 m. 1874 Knut
      Fløgo; Trond 1851-94-43 farmer at Heggjistad; Kari 1854 rtA [to America]; Nils 1856
      farmer at nørdre Fékjo; Halvor 1859 d. [died young]; Halvor 1861-1911-50 unmarried,
      died at Fékjo.
    - Trond took the farm, m. 1884 Kari Ingvarsdtr Grøndalé. Children: twins Anne and Kari
      1884 d. [died]; Halvor 1886-1975 farmer at Heggjistad; Anne 1887 m. 1914 farmer Hans
      Hansen, Hov, Søre Land; Kari 1889-1957 m. Ola Hontvedt, Lærdal (2 boys and 2 girls,
      one dead); Margit 1890-1955 m. Torstein Vøllo d. 1969.
    - As a widow Kari m. 2) widower Sjugurd Larsson Stunde. Children: Mari 1897, Margit 1901.
    - Halvor took over the farm in 1910. He bought Storeluo in 1917 (Stakkestøé had been
      bought in 1878). Farm described as awkward to reach: only a footbridge over the river
      Grøndøla. Halvor cleared most of the land and built new houses at home and on the
      seter. No home seter, but long-distance seters at Føssé and Klanté.
    - "I 1961 selde Halvor garden til Trond Kirkebøen" and built a house in the centre near
      Trøym (see Bjørklund 67-86). Kirkebøen built a new road and vehicle bridge to
      Heggjistad and has planned a cabin field (hyttefelt) on the property.
  p. 392 (Stakkestøé, gnr. 86 bnr. 10): Stakkestøé lay deserted after its people emigrated
    to America in 1880 and was merged with Heggjistad. Storeluo (gnr. 86 bnr. 29) is listed
    as "Nedlagt bruk" (abandoned holding).
  p. 267, under Bjørklund (gnr. 67 bnr. 86): Halvor T. Heggestad 1885-1975 (see Heggestad
    86-9) also lived at Bjørklund. He owned and farmed Heggestad until 1960, when he sold
    it to Trond Kirkebøen, and arranged a house at Bjørklund for his old age. Described as
    kommunemann (local council member) and doms- og skjønsmann (court and valuation juror).
    [Note: 1960 here, 1961 on p. 391; 1885 here, 1886 on p. 391.]
  p. 271, Bygdaheimen (old people's home), residents by year of entry:
    Halvor Heggestad is among those entering in 1975. (The 1974 list includes Ole N. Fekene.)
  p. 355, Flatåker (gnr. 82 bnr. 3, newer dwelling): Torstein N. Vøllo 1882-1969 m. 1930
    Margit Heggestad 1890-1955 [Halvor's full sister]. They bought land from Ola Fékjo and
    built a home there 1935-36, which they called Flatåker. Childless. Sold out of the
    family in 1967 (buyer named in the book; not repeated here).
  p. 357, Rundåkeren (gnr. 82 bnr. 32, new dwelling): Margit Heggestad (86-9), b. 1901
    [Halvor's half-sister], bought a plot at Flatåker and built a house there for herself
    in 1968. Unmarried. A younger couple (named in the book, possibly living, not repeated
    here) built onto the house and lived there from 1973.
    [Note: the book gives 1901; the family gives 8 Dec 1900.]
  Trond Kirkebøen elsewhere in the book: pp. 251, 262, 372, 381, 384, 400. On p. 381
    (Helgeseth/Dekko): the farm was sold to him and laid out as a cabin field with 30
    rental cabins, shop and cafeteria, named Helgeseth Feriesenter; another holding was
    sold to him by Birgit Dekko in 1956. No family tie to the Heggestads is stated.
    Pp. 251, 262, 372, 384, 400 not read.
