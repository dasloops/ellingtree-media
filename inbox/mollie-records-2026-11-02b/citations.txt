KEY POINTS
- Her tribute to her mother (pp. 308-310) is now in as page images, by the owner's leave
  (2 Nov 2026), and is not transcribed. It is in "Family Records of Washington Pioneers
  (Prior to 1891)", Vol. XXIV, DAR of Washington, compiled by Mrs. William Finley Dunlap,
  1953: so it was put into the DAR volumes in her lifetime, the year before she died.
- 1920 census, Los Angeles: Emil, Frances and Alice are all lodgers in the same house
  (dwelling 112), Emil and Frances both in newspaper work.
- 1887: "Mollie Holbrook" witnessed the Coupe heirs' deed of 20 June 1887 (image in).
- Her will and estate, 1954-55: none in Island County. See NOT FOUND.
- City directories put Emil in Oakland in 1912-13 (advertising solicitor, Oakland
  Enquirer, 1913), which supports the 1912 Alameda voter entry being Frances.
- No Carter at 1648 1/2 S. Berendo St. in 1936-38; an Alice P. Carter and a Robert H. Carter
  were at 10382 Rossbury Pl., Los Angeles, in 1930.
