The question: was Mary (Ryan) Dwyer the sister of Cornelius Ryan of Black Brook NY and Bandon,
Renville Co., MN, and if so, which Ryan family: Doon (Mary 18 Mar 1825, Cornelius 10 Jul 1826,
Catherine 1 Apr 1828, children of Patrick Ryan and Catherine Ryan of Commanealine) or Pallasgreen
(Cornelius 31 Oct 1824 and Mary 20 Aug 1826, children of Cornelius Ryan and Bridget Breen)?

KEY POINTS
- Nothing in this batch tells the Doon family from the Pallasgreen family. No record found names
  Cornelius Ryan's parents, a parish, or a sister.
- The Bandon Cornelius Ryan and the Black Brook Cornelius Ryan are the same man. His 1879
  Renville County naturalization rests on a declaration of intention made before the clerk of
  Clinton County, New York, and the Black Brook man's 1862 Clinton County declaration (held,
  batch 4) gives "native of the Co. of Limerick", age 37.
- Cornelius Ryan sold his Black Brook land (lots 42-46, 8th Division, Livingston Patent) on
  13 May 1870. No wife joins the deed or is examined apart, as wives were in the deed above it.
  So he was probably unmarried in May 1870, and married Mary Ann Jane Fox after that.
- A Fox family was at Black Brook in 1850: Thomas Fox, 38, and Eliza, 33, both born Ireland, with
  Mary 9, Jane 7 and Thomas 4, all born New York. Jane, born about 1843, is a candidate for
  Cornelius's wife "Jane Fox, born New York about 1845". Not proved.
- Patrick Ryan of Franklin (stone: born 24 Dec 1812, died 10 Oct 1891) is not shown to be
  Cornelius's brother. His will names his wife Catherine and seven children only, no brother
  or sister, and no Irish place. His children were born in Vermont, Kentucky, Ohio and Iowa
  1847-1859; nothing ties him to Clinton County. His executor, P. H. Kirwan, was also a witness
  at Cornelius's naturalization: a neighbour link, not a family one.
- The 1916 History of Renville County lists Cornelius Ryan among the Irishmen who settled in
  the west part of Bandon in 1869-1870.
- Patrick Dwyer of Butte (died 14 Mar 1910, aged 59) was a son of James and Betsey (Eliza) Dwyer
  of Clinton County: the 1860 Peru census has the family (James 45, Betsey 34, Malachi 12,
  Patrick 10, Jane 7, Bridget 5), and his Salt Lake County probate names his heirs as brother
  James of Keeseville, sister Bridget Curran of Clintonville, sister Jennie Ryan of Westport and
  brother Malachi of Jay. Malachi of Butte (naturalized Essex Co. 1858) is not among them.
