KEY POINTS
- 2 Nov 1821: Israel Clifford "of Corinth in Orange County & State of Vermont" made his will,
  leaving the use of his real estate to his widow Judith while she remained his widow, and then to "my Children Viz Isaac
  Clifford, Moses Clifford, John Clifford, Samuel Clifford, Betsey Thorn, Mary Nutting and
  Desia Swett" (Delia in the 1843 papers), sons taking double shares. Proved at Thetford 29 Jan 1822 (items 1-2).
  This answers batch 3's not-found item B.
- 1843: after Judith's death the land was divided. The court's list of heirs (item 4) gives
  "the Heirs of Moses Clifford deceased", with John "who resides in Pennsylvania", Mary Nutting
  "in York State near Buffalo", Delia Swett "of Washington Vt", and the Thorn children "of
  Tunbridge". Where Moses's heirs lived is partly under an ink blot: "In", then a "d", a
  dotted "i" and an "a" running off the edge of the paper, probably "Indiana" (uncertain; see
  item 4 and judge it from the image).
  The division return (item 6) speaks of an "agent for those heirs that live out of the State".
- 13 Feb 1844: Stephen Eaton was bonded as agent for the property in the district belonging "to
  the heirs or representatives of the heirs of Moses Clifford deceased who was an heir &
  interested in the Estate of Israel Clifford late of Corinth" (item 7). An agent was still
  paying taxes on their 12 acres and leasing the land in 1858 (item 11), so the heirs seem never
  to have come to Vermont to claim it.
- What this does and does not prove. Israel had a son Moses, who was dead by May 1843 and whose
  heirs lived out of Vermont. That fits our Moses: of Washington, Orange County, Vermont, in
  1806-1813 (Washington and Corinth are neighbouring towns, and Israel's daughter Delia lived in
  Washington in 1843); in Indiana from 1825; dead about 1841 (batch 3). No paper in these files
  names Moses's heirs or where they lived, so it is a strong fit, not proof. The Wentworth, NH,
  Moses (b. 1770, son of Isaac and Ruth; batch 2) is a different man in any case: this Moses's
  father is Israel, and Isaac appears here as his brother.
- Israel's widow Judith was the sister of Peter Severance and said Israel enlisted at Deerfield,
  NH, in 1775 (batch 3, item 10); the Deerfield petition of 1779 (batch 2, item 1) is probably the
  same man. Ancestry's tree gives Moses a father Israel born 1728 who died at Corinth in 1807;
  the Israel of these papers died between 2 Nov 1821 and 29 Jan 1822.

  Images:    007714880_00114 (pages 212-213) and 007714880_00115 (pages 214-215)
  Held by:   Vermont Probate Court, Bradford District (Orange County)
  Where:     the will begins at the foot of page 213 and runs onto page 214; the probate follows
             on page 214. The other entries on these pages are other estates.
  Transcription (will):
    Israel Clifford's Will --
    In the Name of God Amen I Israel Clifford of Corinth in Orange County & State of Vermont,
    being weak in body but of sound and perfect mind and memory do make and publish this my last
    Will & Testament, in manner & form following that is to say -- Imprimis, I give to my
    beloved wife Judith Clifford the use & benefit rents & proffits of all my Real estate to
    manage and improve in the way she shall think proper so long as she remains my widow, at the
    expiration of which time I Will & order that my said Real estate shall be divided
    between my Children Viz Isaac Clifford, Moses Clifford, John Clifford, [p. 214] Samuel
    Clifford, Betsey Thorn, Mary Nutting and Desia Swett, to be divided in such manner
    that my sons shall have equal shares and my daughters to have equal shares, and that each
    son shall have double to each daughter -- Item I give to my beloved wife Judith Clifford all
    my personal property of what kind or nature soever after my just debts are paid -- Lastly I
    hereby appoint Stephen Eaton sole Executor of this my last Will & Testament, hereby
    Revoking all former Wills by me made. In witness whereof I do hereunto set my hand & Seal
    this 2d day of November AD 1821 -- The words "improve", "each", and "kind" Interlined before
    signing.
    Signed Sealed published and declared by the above named Israel Clifford to be his Last Will
    and Testament in the presence of us who have hereunto subscribed our names as Witnesses in
    the presence of the Testator      Israel Clifford [L.S.]
    Abel Heath / John Brown 2d / Peter Eaton
  Transcription (probate):
    State of Vermont District of Bradford ss. At a Court of Probate holden at Thetford within &
    for the said district, on the last tuesday of January being the 29th day of said Month, in
    the year of our Lord 1822 -- The foregoing was presented as the last Will & Testament of
    Israel Clifford late of Corinth in sd District deceased, by the Executor therein named for
    Probate; & came under consideration and Abel Heath & Peter Eaton two of the subscribing
    witnesses to the same appearing made Oath that they saw Israel Clifford the Testator Sign &
    Seal & heard him publish & declare the same to be his last Will & Testament and that he was
    of sound and disposing mind & memory when he did it, & that they together with John Brown 2d
    all signed as witnesses to the same, at the same time in the presence of the said Testator,
    and of each other -- Whereupon it is considered that the same be & hereby is proved,
    approved, ratified and confirmed as the last Will & Testament of said deceased.
                                         Elisha Thayer Judge of Probate
  Notes:     The record book clearly reads "Desia"; the 1843 papers call her "Delia Swett" /
             "Delia Sweat wife of John Sweat". Ancestry indexes her as "Desia Sweet" here and "Delila Irvett" in
             the estate file. The loose original will is in the estate file (record 664830, below) and was
             not uploaded.

9. Israel Clifford estate file, 12 images: Ancestry 9084, record
   https://www.ancestry.com/search/collections/9084/records/664830, images 004478860_00268
   to _00279.
   - Warrant to appraise, 29 Jan 1822, Elisha Thayer, judge, to Abel Heath, Peter Eaton and
     Ephraim Ward of Corinth: estate "of Israel Clifford deceased, who lately died at Corinth",
     shown by Stephen Eaton, executor (image 11).
   - Inventory, dated at Corinth 2 Feb 1822: real estate "the undivided two thirds of about one
     hundred and thirty acres of land and the buildings thereon" in Corinth, $844.44, and "one
     half of a pew in the methodist meeting house in Vershire", $11; personal estate including
     oxen, cows, eleven sheep, blacksmith tools, joiner's tools, a loom, books, a clock and 26
     barrels of cider; notes against Peter Robinson, Samuel Thorn, Abel Heath and others; total
     $1547.44 (images 5-6; the endorsement, image 4, reads "Vershire Feby 26th 1822 Accepted &
     ordered to be Recorded").
   - Executor's account, "the account of Stephen Eaton Executor on the Estate of Israel Clifford
     Late of Corinth in Bradford Probate district Deceased", Jan 1822 to June 1823 (images 2-3):
     journey to Thetford to prove the will, 29 Jan 1822; "trying to settle with Saml Thorn";
     costs of a suit against Thorn at the Chelsea county court, June term 1822, and of
     depositions for it; visits to "Mrs Cliffords to settle with Thorn" and "to let out Farm";
     a journey to Fairlee "for order to Notify Heirs and all Concerned to attend settlement";
     personal estate delivered to "Judith Clifford (Widow of said Deceased)" per her receipt,
     $643.50; signed Stephen Eaton, executor.
