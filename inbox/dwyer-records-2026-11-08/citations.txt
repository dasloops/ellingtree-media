KEY POINTS
- No record found names John Dwyer's or Mary Ryan's parish, townland or parents. The question is not settled.
- The Keeseville registers (Immaculate Conception and St. Jean-Baptiste, films 7857889, 7857890, 7857892) are padlocked
  for this account at home; the Seguin transcription is a 2007 book on the library shelf only. Item 1 could not be read.
- Port Henry's filmed registers begin in 1863; no Catholic register is catalogued for Canajoharie. Item 2 not found.
- Four John Dwyer x Mary Ryan marriages fall in 1836-1850 in the Irish index, all in Co. Tipperary, none in Co. Limerick:
  Boherlahan 10 Feb 1839 (groom "P. Clonoulty"); Anacarty and Donohill 4 Feb 1845 (Carhue, with a consanguinity
  dispensation); Knockavilla 23 May 1847 ("Mary Ryan of Kilshinane"); Clonoulty 14 Nov 1849 (the index says 14 Sep).
- A John Dwyer and Mary Ryan of Carhue baptized Bridget at Anacarty on 17 Aug 1848 (sponsors James Ryan, Mary Carr[?]).
  By the brief's own test, that couple was still in Ireland in Aug 1848.
- Ellen, of John Dwyer "Hnd[?]" and Mary Ryan, of Kilshinane, was baptized at Knockavilla in June 1847, one month after
  the Kilshinane marriage. A child of a John Dwyer and Mary Ryan was baptized there in Jan 1849 (name and townland lost).
- Cappawhite: Mary, daughter of Connor Ryan and Kitty Darcy of Kilbeg, was baptized 21 MARCH 1825, not 21 February
  (the entry follows an explicit "March 10" under the heading "March 1825"); sponsors Patt Ryan and Julian Darcy.
  The index also has John (1829), Connor (1833) and Susan (1838) to the same parents at Cappawhite.
- Philip Dwire of Black Brook (1850: Philip 35, Mary 31, John 11, Michael 9, Thomas 6, Patrick 1, all born Ireland)
  matches a Philip Dwyer and Mary Shanahan of Glengar[?], Doon, Co. Limerick, whose sons John (26 Nov 1838), Michael
  (2 May 1841) and Thomas (20 Aug 1844) were baptized there. A Thos Fitzgerald sponsored John at Doon in 1838; a Thomas
  Fitzgerald, 37, collier, is Philip's next neighbour at Black Brook in 1850. The Doon register of Dec 1838 also names
  Pickhams (Biddy, Nelly and Ned Pickham). None of this names John Dwyer; it places Philip's family at Doon.
- 1900, Butte: John Dalton's household at 312 East Granite holds Kate (mother of 6, 6 living, her parents born Ireland)
  and a roomer James J. Dwyer, 17, born June 1883 New York. No older Mary Dwyer is in the house.
- Malachi Dwyer's Montana death certificate (7 Dec 1899) gives his birthplace as Ireland, not New York; no parents.
