KEY POINTS
- Doon has a Ryan family with both a Cornelius and a Mary of the right ages: Mary, baptized
  18 Mar 1825, and Cornelius, baptized 10 Jul 1826, children of Patrick Ryan and Catherine Ryan,
  of the same townland (read on the NLI image as Commondulea [?] / Commonalin [?]). Cornelius's
  sponsors were Cornelius and Mary Ryan.
- Pallasgreen, next to Doon, has a second such pair: Cornelius, 31 Oct 1824, and Mary, 20 Aug 1826,
  children of Cornelius Ryan and Bridget Breen. Neither pair is proved to be ours.
- Doon also has Malachy, baptized 4 Dec 1831, son of Malachy Dwyer and Margaret Ryan, with a sister
  Mary baptized 5 Dec 1828, same townland (Garthoun [?]). He is the only Malachy Dwyer baptized
  1826-1837 in the Co. Limerick or Co. Tipperary index whose age fits Malachi of Butte.
- Cornelius Ryan was in Renville County, Minnesota, by July 1870: a Cornelius Ryan, born Ireland,
  is in the 1870 census of the Town of Camp, Renville, alone, next to Timothy Carline, and the
  tract book shows a Cornelius Ryan claim on section 8 in Aug 1870 beside Carline's land. The
  census gives his age as 25, which does not fit a man born about 1825-29.
- The Bandon Cornelius Ryan took a homestead (W half NE quarter, section 8, T112 R33) on
  14 Jul 1873 and proved it 9 Jul 1880 (final certificate 1988; patent 10 May 1882).
- His wife's maiden name was Fox: two of his children's death records name the mother as
  Mary Ann Fox and Jane Fox. He is buried at St. Patrick Catholic Cemetery, Franklin, Renville
  County, death date not recorded on the memorial. No Dwyer is recorded in that cemetery.
- Malachi Dwyer was naturalized in Essex County, New York, on 10 Oct 1858 (Butte Great Register,
  1899). A Patrick Dwyer, 47, born New York, registered at the same address, 241 E. Granite, Butte,
  in March 1899.
