4 files. All images are FamilySearch's full-size originals (dist.jpg). Records found on Ancestry

KEY POINTS
- Kate (Dwyer) Dalton's Montana death certificate, 1908, names her father John Dwyer, born
  Ireland, and her mother's maiden name Mary Ryan, born Ireland. She was born in New York.
- John J. Dwyer's Montana marriage license, 1905, names his parents John Dwyer and Mary Dwyer,
  maiden name Mary Ryan. His bride, Sarah A. Corbett, was born at Black Brook, New York.
- Mary Ann Dwyer's Vermont marriage card, 1879 (Ancestry only), says she was born at Moriah, N.Y.,
  daughter of John Dwyer and Mary (Ryan).
- Minnesota census, 1885, Birch Cooley: John Dwyer 71 and Mary 62, both born Ireland, with a girl
  Mary J. Ryan, 2, born Minnesota, listed directly after them.
- Minnesota census, 1885, Bandon township (next to Birch Cooley): Cornelius Ryan, 57, born Ireland,
  no wife listed, with seven children, the youngest Mary Jane, 3. This is very likely the same
  child counted twice. In 1875 this Cornelius Ryan ("Ragen" in the index) had a wife Anna, 30,
  born New York; in 1880 a wife Jane, 35. His children were all born in Minnesota from about 1872.
- Whether this Cornelius Ryan is the Cornelius Ryan of Black Brook (native of Co. Limerick, 37 in
  1862; sold lots 42-46 to the Rogers company in May 1870) is NOT proved. The ages agree within
  three years and the timing fits, but no record yet ties them.
- Cornelius S. Dwyer's household in the 1895 Minnesota census (Norfolk, Renville) has no parent
  living with it. No 1895 record of John or Mary Dwyer was found anywhere in Minnesota.
