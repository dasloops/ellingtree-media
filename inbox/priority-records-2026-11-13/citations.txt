  Notes:     The name is spelled "Chesman" in the register and "Cheesman" in the index.
             The register does not name her parents ("Not Known"), so it neither proves nor
             disproves Pickett Marvin. Her age is given as 84 years 0 months 24 days, which works
             back to 31 May 1807, five years later than the 1 June 1802 the archive has. The day
             and month are a day apart, so the informant knew her birthday but perhaps not the
             year. Worth weighing against the censuses.
