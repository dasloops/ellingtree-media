KEY POINTS
1. 1850, Lenox, Ashtabula Co., Ohio: Walter Allee 54, farmer, b. N.Y.; Cybil 54, b. N.Y.; Melinda 14,
   b. Pa.; Zalmon 11 and Harriet 7, b. Ohio. In the same dwelling: Daniel Piper 29 (Vt.) and Miranda
   Piper 30 (N.Y.) with Delos 6, Emery 4, Genilla 9/12. The page states no relationships.
2. Melinda's birthplace (Pa., c. 1836) fits the family's stay at Springfield, Erie Co., Pa., c. 1836.
3. 1840 Jefferson and 1830 Hopewell: Walter's household tallies match a couple born 1790s with
   children born 1815-1840. The 1830 entry is spelled "Walter Alley".
4. Four index entries record the marriage of Albert Allee and Lucinda Sargent/Sargeant, Will Co.,
   Ill., in 1851, dated 3 June, 17 December (two entries) and 25 December. One entry gives ages 21
   and 18. The register images (DGS 007746986, images 655-658) are padlocked.
5. 1850, Will Co., Ill.: Lucinda Sargent 21 lives with Enoch Sargent 50 at Sherburne, and an "Albert
   Allen" 23, mason, b. N.Y., boards at a hotel in Momence. Both are possible identifications only
   (see Notes). Lucinda's birthplace there reads "Ia.", not Indiana.
6. 1875, Reynolds, Todd Co., Minn.: Albert 49, Lucinda 45 (b. Ind.; her father b. Md., mother b. Va.),
   and Emery, Eva, Emma J., Elsie, Walter, "Levi E." (Louis) and Ella B.
7. 1885, Long Prairie village: Albert with Sarah Allee 61, b. Ky., and Walter, Lewis and Ella. So
   the second marriage took place before 29 June 1885.
8. 1895: Albert 68, stone mason, Civil War soldier, with Sarah 70, b. Ky. 1900: Albert 73, widowed,
   with Richard and Agnes E. Fearn at Lake Emma, Hubbard Co. Ella B. Allee, 26, single, is in the
   same household. The next dwelling is Alonzo [?] Partlow, 23, b. Mich., and the one after that is
   Louis E. and Carrie B. Allee with Everett, Ila May and Guy.
9. Pension index cards: Albert's reads "D 42 Minn. Inf." [sic], filed 29 Aug 1885, invalid,
   certificate 373,739. Daniel's reads "D 42 Ill. Inf.; Unass'd, 3 Minn. Inf.". His widow Clarissa
   L. M. Allee filed on 19 May 1913, so Daniel died before that date.
   1910: Albert, 83, is with the Fearns at Clay Twp, marked "M2" (second marriage), married 25 years.
   That puts the marriage to Sarah at about 1884-85. 1920: Albert, 92, widowed, with the Fearns at
   Park Rapids.
10. Walter's estate was NOT found in the Ashtabula Co. general probate index (see the last section).
