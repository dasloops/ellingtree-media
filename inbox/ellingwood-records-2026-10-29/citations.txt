    Signed Sealed & Delivered in Presence of        Israel Ellinwood (seal)

             children Israel (of Boston, coaster, guardian of his brother Daniel), Ralph,
             Daniel, Esther and Abiel (wife of Richard Thistle 2d). Sarah West, named in the
             1743 division the brief quotes, does not sign here. Matches the task on the
             widow, her remarriage and the children. This is the acquittance for the
             personal estate, not the division of the real estate itself.

  Notes:     This book is a later fair copy of Andover's early marriages, in a 19th-century
