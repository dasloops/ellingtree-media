KEY POINTS
- Ralph Ellinwood's will of 7 Jan 1673[/4] and inventory of 30 Jan 1673[/4] (the record-book
  copy) name wife Hellen/Ellinor and children Ralph, John, Joseph, Benjamin, David, Mary and
  Elizabeth, and give Ralph "the ten acres which was formerly old Robert Moultons lott".
- The Salem court book has the probate (30 June 1674) on the page facing a fine on "William
  Basse alias Bath" for drunkenness, and in June 1677 both "Martin Hale, miler of Beverly &
  Ralph Ellenwood his man, for taking too much toll" and Hall v. Wm Bath and "Elenor his wife,
  relict of Ralph Ellenwood".
- Ralph Jr.'s original will, both pages, is dated 8 April 1741, signed by mark, witnessed by
  Israel Wood, John Stephens and Anthony Wood, and proved 11 May 1741 (docket 8712); it names
  "my daughter Returne Wardwell". The 1755 division assigns share 5 "To Return now Wardel widw".
- The third Ralph (d. 1728/9) is "Ralph Ellinwood Junior late of Beverly Mariner" in his
  inventory of 22 Jan 1728/9, which includes shares in two unnamed coasting sloops; his widow
  administers as "Sarah Ellenwood alias Allen" (docket 8711).
- His children are named together in the 1733 distribution, the 1743 acquittance and the
  1755 dower division: Israel (eldest, of Boston, coaster, in 1743), Ralph, Daniel, Sarah (alias
  West, later wife of How), Esther (later wife of Edmund Giles) and Abiel/Abia (wife of
  Richard Thistle).
- Joseph Herrick of Topsfield was made guardian of Israel and Ralph on 13 March 1731/2.
- Elijah Ellingwood and Abigail Rice: license 19 July 1841, married 25 July 1841 by R.
  Hargrave, both of Dearborn County.
- Several items are later copies or printed texts, not the originals; each entry says which.
