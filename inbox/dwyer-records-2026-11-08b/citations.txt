KEY POINTS
- Still no record names John Dwyer's or Mary Ryan's parish, townland or parents.
- Three of John's Black Brook neighbours declared Irish counties:
  Cornelius Ryan, "native of the Co. of Limerick Ireland", aged 37, 22 Oct 1862;
  William Pickham, "native of the County of Limerick Ireland", aged 30, 13 Jun 1854;
  Thomas Russell, John's naturalization witness in 1860, "County of Tipperary Ireland", aged 30, 1852/1854.
- Philip Dwyer's naturalization (declared 27 Oct 185[9?], admitted 17 Oct 1866) says only "Ireland", aged 40.
  Thomas Dwyer of Black Brook, aged 21, came to the U.S. as a minor of 5, admitted the same day, 17 Oct 1866.
- 1850 Black Brook: two Pickham households (John Pickham, 38, and William Pickham, 28, both born Ireland) on the
  same enumeration as Philip Dwire. John Pickham's children William (6) and Mary were born in Canada.
- Pickhams were a Doon, Co. Limerick, family in the Irish index (several married Ryans); a William Pickham married an
  Ellen Morrisson at Doon on 28 Jun 1846; William Pickham of Black Brook has a wife Ellen, aged 26 in 1850.
  The identity is not proven.
- 1855 New York census, Moriah: Malachi Dwyer, 22, born Ireland, married, one year in the town, laborer, alien,
  with Bridget, 24. Malachi J. Dwyer who died at Butte in 1899 was 68, born Ireland. Kinship to John is unknown.
- John Dwyer's own 1855 household is not indexed at Moriah under any spelling; Clinton County's 1865 census returns
  no entries in the index.
- A further John Dwyer x Mary Ryan marriage is indexed in the period: Carrick-on-Suir, 6 Jun 1846 (Latin entry,
  index only). No marriage of the pair is indexed at Doon or in any Co. Limerick parish, and no Dwyer groom appears
  among the 30 Mary Ryan marriages indexed at Doon 1839-1847.

             of the parties of the first part" in 1866-67. The Black Brook Cornelius's 1860 and 1870 households
